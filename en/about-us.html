Who are we?

What is 1 UP

A simple idea: that communicating what you do doesn't have to be an impossible level.

1UP was created to support those who have something valuable to offer but don’t always know how to convey it. Because knowing your work doesn’t mean it’s easy to explain it on a website, in a publication, or in a conversation with a potential client.

I'm Alejandra Merlo and I have nearly 15 years of experience in marketing, communications, and design. During that time, I worked for companies and organizations. Over a year ago, due to life circumstances, I embarked on my independent journey, helping brands and entrepreneurs communicate their value more clearly and effectively. From content creation to teaching and digital marketing work, I found myself collaborating on a project for entrepreneurs that led me to take the plunge and build my own brand.

While I use AI responsibly, my brain is still in charge when it comes to creating ideas and solutions that are authentic and useful. For example, all the text you see on this website was written by me. And that’s how I’ll help you grow your brand without having to rely 100% on AI.

I invite you to take another shot at the game and level up your communication. Write to me!

P.S.: Yes, I wrote “WE” because I have collaborators if your brand needs something more specific, such as social media management, paid media, or graphic design.
